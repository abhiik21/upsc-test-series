Privacy Policy

How account, usage and support information is handled on the platform.

Last updated: 19 September 2026
On this page1. Information We Collect2. How Information Is Used3. Test and Performance Data4. Payments5. Service Providers6. Data Retention7. Your Choices8. Contact

1. Information We Collect

Depending on how you use the platform, we may collect information such as name, email address, mobile number, account details, subscription information, test attempts, responses, results, bookmarks, saved resources and support enquiries.

2. How Information Is Used

Information may be used to create and manage accounts, provide tests and content, calculate results and analytics, process payments, send service notifications, respond to support requests, maintain security and improve the platform.

3. Test and Performance Data

Responses, scores, timing and related activity may be stored so that the platform can generate result reports, rankings where applicable, progress summaries and personalised practice features.

4. Payments

Payment transactions may be processed through third-party payment providers. The platform may receive transaction identifiers and payment status required to confirm an order, while payment credentials are handled according to the provider’s systems and policies.

5. Service Providers

We may use trusted service providers for hosting, storage, analytics, communications, payment processing, security and similar functions necessary to operate the platform.

6. Data Retention

Information may be retained for as long as reasonably necessary for account administration, service delivery, legal obligations, dispute resolution, security and legitimate operational purposes.

7. Your Choices

Depending on applicable law and the platform’s capabilities, you may request access, correction or deletion of certain account information. Some data may need to be retained for legal, security or transaction purposes.

8. Contact

For privacy questions or requests, please use the Contact Us page.

These pages are a product draft for the website and should be reviewed and adapted to the actual business, jurisdiction, payment terms, data practices and legal requirements before publication.
