Frequently asked questions

Key details about the test series.

How many tests are included?

The programme is structured as 25 tests, combining subject-wise practice, mixed practice and full-length simulations.

What is the marking scheme?

The full-length tests are designed around the UPSC Prelims GS Paper-I pattern with negative marking. The exact marks configuration is displayed before each test.

Will I get detailed solutions?

Yes. Eligible tests include answer explanations and supporting sources so that you can review both correct and incorrect responses.

Can I see my rank and percentile?

Where ranking is enabled, the result page shows your relative position among eligible participants after the test.

Can the test sequence change?

Yes. Test release dates and the final sequence can be updated by the platform administrator as the programme develops.
