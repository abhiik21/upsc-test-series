Frequently asked questions

Common questions about the test series.

What is included in the Prelims Test Series?

The series is planned to include subject-wise tests, full-length simulations, detailed solutions and performance analytics.

Can I attempt tests on mobile?

The website is designed to be responsive and suitable for desktop, tablet and mobile screens.

Are detailed solutions provided?

Yes. The test experience is designed to provide explanations after submission so that you can review each question.

Can I retake a test?

Retake availability can be configured by the platform for each individual test or test series.

Is the payment one-time or recurring?

Plans are one-time payments for a fixed access period. Nothing renews automatically.

What happens after I pay?

Once the payment is confirmed, your plan is activated on your account and the premium tests unlock.
